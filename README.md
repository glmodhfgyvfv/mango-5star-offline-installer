## About

Mango 5Star is a business quality management and compliance platform for creating, managing, and distributing standard operating procedures, checklists, and compliance documents. Features document control, version management, employee acknowledgment tracking, and audit trail capabilities. Designed for small to medium businesses needing ISO-compliant documentation management without enterprise software complexity.

## Common Problems & Limitations

- **Commercial License** — Subscription pricing for business use
- **Niche Use Case** — Primarily for businesses with formal quality management requirements
- **Learning Curve** — Quality management concepts and software configuration require training
- **Integration Scope** — Integrations with other business software limited compared to enterprise platforms
